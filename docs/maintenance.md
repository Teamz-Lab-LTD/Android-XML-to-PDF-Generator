# Maintenance and support

Maintained by Teamz Lab on a best-effort basis. No response-time SLA is implied by a badge, release or open issue.

Priority order: data loss/corruption or crashes, broken installation, OS compatibility, reproducible rendering regressions, documentation. New adapters and large features need a concrete use case and tests. A feature request is not a delivery commitment.

CI validates changes; Dependabot proposes dependency updates monthly. Updates are reviewed and tested before merging. It does not automatically approve, publish, message users or change other projects. Physical-device evidence is recorded when those devices are available.

For a report: identify the export path, provide the exact commit/version and OS, and share a minimal reproduction without private data. For sponsored maintenance or app integration, contact [Teamz Lab](https://teamzlab.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=opensource&utm_campaign=layout_to_pdf#contact).
